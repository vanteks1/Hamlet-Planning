# Regional Road Frontage & Access Requirements

This document outlines the road frontage, shared driveway, and private road construction standards for the primary counties in the Atlanta NE/Duluth region.

---

## 1. Barrow County
*   **Minimum Frontage (R-1/AG):** 100 feet (on existing public road).
*   **Frontage (Cul-de-sac):** 50 feet.
*   **Shared Driveways:** Allowed for a maximum of **2 lots**. Serving 3+ lots requires a Variance or a Major Subdivision road.
*   **Private Road Standards:** 
    *   **ROW:** 60 feet.
    *   **Pavement:** 24 feet wide (back-of-curb to back-of-curb). 
    *   **Surface:** Must be **paved (asphalt)** for Major Subdivisions.
    *   **Cul-de-sac:** 40-foot pavement radius / 50-foot ROW radius.

## 2. Gwinnett County
*   **Minimum Frontage (RA-200/R-100):** 40 feet.
*   **Shared Driveways:** Limited to **2 single-family residences**.
*   **Private Road Standards:**
    *   **ROW:** 46 feet (entrance); 42 feet (interior).
    *   **Pavement:** 24 feet (entrance); 20 feet (interior).
    *   **Surface:** Must be **paved** to county standards.

## 3. Jackson County
*   **Minimum Frontage:** 60 feet.
*   **Frontage (Cul-de-sac):** 35 feet.
*   **Shared Driveways:** Serves no more than **2 lots/buildings**. If serving 3+, it must be a named private road meeting public standards.
*   **Private Road Standards:**
    *   **ROW:** 50 feet minimum.
    *   **Surface:** Must meet public standards if serving more than 2 lots (paved).

## 4. Hall County
*   **Minimum Frontage (Local Street):** 50 feet.
*   **Minimum Frontage (Collector/Arterial):** 150 feet.
*   **Shared Driveways:** Allowed for a maximum of **2 parcels** via a recorded access easement.
*   **Private Road Standards:**
    *   **ROW:** 60 feet (ditched); 50 feet (curbed).
    *   **Pavement:** 20 feet minimum.

## 5. Forsyth County
*   **Minimum Frontage:** 60 feet.
*   **Frontage (Cul-de-sac):** 20 feet.
*   **Shared Driveways:** Allowed for **2 parcels**. Common access easements required.
*   **Private Road Standards:** Must be constructed to current county standards (paved). Flag lot "poles" must be 60 feet wide.

## 6. North Fulton (Milton/Alpharetta)
*   **Minimum Frontage (Milton AG-1):** 35 feet.
*   **Lot Width:** Milton requires **150 feet** at the building line for AG-1 lots.
*   **Shared Driveways:** Typically limited to **3 single-family lots**.
*   **Private Road Standards:** Must be maintained by a mandatory HOA and built to Fulton County standards.

---

## Regional Trends & Strategy Notes
1.  **The "Two-Lot" Rule:** Most counties (Barrow, Gwinnett, Jackson, Hall, Forsyth) strictly limit shared driveways to **2 lots**. Attempting to serve 4 lots from one driveway will almost always require a **Variance** or a full **Major Subdivision** road.
2.  **Gravel vs. Paved:** For any new development involving a "road" (Major Subdivision), **paving is mandatory**. Gravel is typically only permitted for single-family driveways or very low-density rural tracts (often 5+ acres per lot) via special permit.
3.  **Variance Precedent:** When searching for land with narrow frontage (like 857 Laurel Ln), always check for recent **Board of Appeals** cases in that county. A successful variance for a neighbor (like BOA 2025-009) is the best indicator of success for our 4-lot hamlet.
