# ADU & MIL Suite Regulatory Guide

Two stakeholders (Erica and Gabi) have explicitly stated that a Mother-in-Law (MIL) suite is a hard requirement. This document maps out what each county actually allows, since the rules vary dramatically — from fully detached cottages to attached-only suites with strict size caps.

---

## Key Terminology

- **ADU (Accessory Dwelling Unit):** A secondary residential unit on the same parcel as a primary home. May be detached (cottage/carriage house) or attached (in-law suite, basement apartment).
- **MIL Suite:** Common term for an attached secondary unit within or directly connected to the primary dwelling. Usually not a separate structure.
- **DADU (Detached ADU):** A freestanding secondary structure (separate building) on the same lot.
- **Guest Cottage:** Some counties use this term for a detached structure. May prohibit full kitchen or limit to temporary occupancy — read the fine print.

---

## County-by-County ADU Rules

### 1. Barrow County
- **ADU Allowed?** Yes, in R-1 and AG zones.
- **Type Permitted:** Attached only (within the primary structure or an attached garage conversion). Detached structures are classified as second dwellings and are **not permitted** on a single-family lot without a variance.
- **Size Limit:** The lesser of **50% of the primary home's heated sq. ft.** or 1,000 sq. ft.
- **Bedroom Limit:** Typically 2 bedrooms max.
- **Kitchen:** Full kitchen is allowed if attached.
- **Owner Occupancy:** Owner must occupy either the primary or the ADU.
- **Permit Required?** Yes — building permit + separate septic sizing review.
- **Verdict for Hamlet:** Detached cottages for Erica/Gabi are **NOT permitted** under current code without a variance. Each family's MIL suite must be integrated into their main house design.

### 2. Jackson County
- **ADU Allowed?** Yes, in AG and R-1 zones.
- **Type Permitted:** Both attached and **detached** ADUs are allowed in AG zones. Detached structures must meet setbacks and be subordinate in size to the primary dwelling.
- **Size Limit:** Max **1,200 sq. ft.** for detached ADU.
- **Bedroom Limit:** 2 bedrooms max (detached); no firm limit (attached).
- **Kitchen:** Full kitchen allowed.
- **Owner Occupancy:** Required.
- **Permit Required?** Yes — building permit + separate septic or EH sign-off for additional bedroom load.
- **Verdict for Hamlet:** **Most flexible** of the target counties. Detached cottages are viable here. This is a significant advantage for Erica and Gabi if they want a fully separate structure.

### 3. Hall County
- **ADU Allowed?** Yes, as "accessory structures" in R-1/AG zones.
- **Type Permitted:** Detached ADUs are permitted but must be **on the same lot** as the primary dwelling. Cannot be subdivided off separately.
- **Size Limit:** Max **1,500 sq. ft.** or 50% of primary home (whichever is less).
- **Bedroom Limit:** 2 bedrooms.
- **Kitchen:** Full kitchen allowed (Hall is relatively permissive).
- **Owner Occupancy:** Required in most cases; check if AG zone exempts this.
- **Permit Required?** Yes.
- **Verdict for Hamlet:** Good option — detached cottages are allowed. Confirm AG vs. R-1 zoning on the specific parcel, as rules may differ.

### 4. Gwinnett County
- **ADU Allowed?** Limited. Gwinnett is generally restrictive about secondary units on single-family lots.
- **Type Permitted:** Attached "family member suites" are allowed. Detached ADUs are essentially not permitted in standard R-100/R-75 zones without a variance.
- **Size Limit:** 650 sq. ft. max for attached family suites in some zones.
- **Bedroom Limit:** 1 bedroom.
- **Kitchen:** Full kitchen may be prohibited in some zones (wet bar only).
- **Owner Occupancy:** Required.
- **Permit Required?** Yes, and scrutinized.
- **Verdict for Hamlet:** **Least favorable.** Gwinnett's restrictions on ADUs make the MIL suite requirement difficult to satisfy, especially for detached units. Strongly deprioritize Gwinnett parcels.

### 5. Forsyth County
- **ADU Allowed?** Yes, with conditions.
- **Type Permitted:** Attached ADUs broadly permitted. Detached ADUs allowed in AG zones but require a **Special Use Permit (SUP)** in some residential zones.
- **Size Limit:** Max **1,200 sq. ft.** or 50% of primary home.
- **Bedroom Limit:** 2 bedrooms.
- **Kitchen:** Full kitchen allowed.
- **Owner Occupancy:** Required.
- **Permit Required?** Yes; detached ADU in AG may require SUP application ($500–$800 fee + 6–8 week review).
- **Verdict for Hamlet:** Workable but adds process for detached cottages. Plan for SUP timeline if targeting Forsyth with DADU requirement.

## ADU Type Decision Matrix

| Stakeholder Need | Best County Match | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Attached MIL suite (any county) | All counties | Fewest hurdles universally |
| Detached cottage (<1,200 sq. ft.) | **Jackson** (easiest), Hall, Forsyth | Barrow and Gwinnett require variance |
| Detached cottage (>1,200 sq. ft.) | **Hall** (up to 1,500 sq. ft.) | Only county with higher size cap |
| Separate rental income unit | Verify rental language per county | Most counties restrict short-term rental of ADUs |

## Impact on House Design

| Approach | Build Cost | Flexibility | Septic Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 large houses with integrated MIL suites | Lower per unit | Less privacy for MIL | Combined system, 1 perc test per lot |
| 4 smaller houses + 4 detached cottages | Higher (8 structures total) | Maximum privacy | May require 2 perc tests per lot |
| Hybrid: 2 with attached, 2 with detached | Moderate | Tailored to each family | Mixed approach |

---

## Pre-Offer Checklist

- [ ] Confirm current ADU ordinance with the county planning office (codes updated frequently post-COVID).
- [ ] Ask specifically: "Is a detached ADU with a full kitchen permitted by-right in [zone]?"
- [ ] If SUP required, get the timeline and fee schedule before committing.
- [ ] Verify that the **septic system** can be designed to accommodate the ADU bedroom count from day one.
- [ ] Check HOA covenants (if any) — even if the county allows ADUs, a prior HOA may prohibit them.

---

## Strategy Notes

1. **Jackson County is the clear winner** for detached MIL suites without bureaucratic overhead. Prioritize AG-zoned Jackson parcels for Erica and Gabi's lots.
2. **Design for the ADU from day one.** Trying to add a MIL suite after the primary house is permitted often triggers a new permit, new septic review, and new setback analysis. Build the full program into the initial application.
3. **"Guest house" vs. "ADU":** Some counties allow a "guest house" (no kitchen) more liberally than a full ADU. If full kitchen is not essential, this may open options in Barrow or Gwinnett — but confirm with EH that a guest house still triggers a bedroom-count increase for septic.
