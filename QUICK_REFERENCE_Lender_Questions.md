# Lender Interview Guide: Land Acquisition & Subdivision

When purchasing land like the Rockbridge Road property with the intent to subdivide, you are looking for a **Commercial Land Loan** or a specialized **Lot Loan**. Use these questions to ensure the lender's terms align with the Hamlet project goals.

## Core Subdivision Questions

1. **Partial Release Clause:** 
   * "Will you include a 'Partial Release Clause' in the Security Deed? If so, what is the required 'Release Price' per lot (e.g., 120% of the pro-rata loan balance)?"
   * *Why this matters:* This allows you to legally separate the 4 lots so each family can eventually own their portion individually without the bank holding a lien over the whole 8+ acres.

2. **Lender’s Consent on Plats:**
   * "Is your bank comfortable signing the 'Lender's Consent' block on a Gwinnett County Final Plat? What is your internal process and typical turnaround time for reviewing and signing a plat?"
   * *Why this matters:* Gwinnett won't record the subdivision without the lender's notarized signature.

3. **Loan-to-Value (LTV) Requirements:**
   * "What is the maximum LTV you allow for raw land? If we subdivide, will you require a new appraisal of the 'remaining' land to ensure the LTV stays within limits?"
   * *Why this matters:* If the value of the remaining land drops too low after a lot is released, they may block the release.

4. **Construction-to-Permanent Transition:**
   * "Once the land is subdivided, do you offer construction loans to the individual lot owners? If so, can the land equity be used as the down payment for those individual construction loans?"

## Financial Terms & Flexibility

5. **Interest-Only Period:**
   * "Do you offer an interest-only period during the first 12–24 months while we are in the 'soft cost' phase (permitting/zoning)?"
   * *Why this matters:* This keeps your monthly carry cost low while you are still paying your current mortgage.

6. **Release of Personal Guarantees:**
   * "If multiple families are on the loan, can an individual be released from the master land loan once their specific lot is subdivided and they secure their own construction financing?"

7. **Prepayment Penalties:**
   * "Are there penalties for paying off large portions of the principal early? (Specifically when we subdivide and 'buy out' individual lots)."
