# Project Plan: Hamlet Land Acquisition & Development

## Phase 1: Search & Identification
1.  **Search Criteria:**
    - Minimum 2.5-3.0 acres.
    - **Primary Constraints:**
      - Within 1.25 hours of the Downtown MARTA Railyard.
      - Within 40 minutes of the Mall of Georgia (Buford).
    - **Targeted Cities:** Duluth, Suwanee, Buford, Sugar Hill, Braselton, Hoschton, Dacula, Lawrenceville, Flowery Branch, Cumming.
    - Zoning: Residential (R-X) with favorable subdivision history.
2.  **Resources:**
    - Zillow, Redfin, LandWatch, LandAndFarm.
    - Gwinnett/Fulton Tax Commissioner (qPublic.net).
    - FEMA Flood Map Service Center.

## Phase 2: Due Diligence (Evaluation)
1.  **Zoning Verification:**
    - Confirm "Major Subdivision" vs "Minor Subdivision" (4+ lots often triggers Major).
    - Check "Successive Subdivision" rules (2-year rule).
2.  **Infrastructure:**
    - Sewer accessibility (critical for lot splits < 1 acre).
    - Septic soil testing (if sewer not available).
    - Utility access (Water, Electricity, Gas, Internet).
3.  **Physical Constraints:**
    - Topography (steep slopes increase build costs).
    - Flood zones & stream buffers (3-phase Erosion & Sediment Control).
    - Tree preservation ordinances (Atlanta area is strict).

## Phase 3: Acquisition
1.  **Offer:** Negotiate contingencies for survey and soil testing.
2.  **Closing:** Ensure clear title and no restrictive covenants preventing subdivision.

## Phase 4: Subdivision & Permitting
1.  **Survey & Concept Plat:** Hire a licensed Georgia surveyor.
2.  **LDP (Land Disturbance Permit):** Mandatory for common developments.
3.  **Public Hearings:** Required for Major Subdivisions (NPU/Planning Commission).

## Phase 5: Infrastructure & Construction
1.  Install shared driveway (if required by county).
2.  Run utilities to each lot.
3.  Begin individual home construction.

## Critical Resources
- **qPublic.net:** Property history and tax data.
- **FEMA Flood Center:** Verification of flood risk.
- **Municode:** Local zoning code lookup.
- **Georgia EPD:** Erosion and sediment control guidelines.
