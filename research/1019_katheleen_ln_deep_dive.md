## Zoning & Subdivision Feasibility
- **Current Zoning:** **A2 (Rural Estate)**
    - **Note on qPublic:** qPublic displays a legacy label of **"MH"** and a tax classification of **"R3"**. 
    - **Verification:** The "MH" (Manufactured Home) district was officially **rescinded by Walton County on Dec 3, 2002**. All such parcels are now regulated under the **A2** district standards.
    - **Classification:** The **"R3"** label on qPublic is a **Tax Assessor classification** (Residential Lot < 5 acres), not a zoning designation.
- **Min Lot Size (Septic + Public Water):** **1.0 Acre** (Updated Jan 2024 via Errata #2/3)
- **Min Lot Size (Septic + Well):** **2.0 Acres**
- **Feasibility:** 
    - **4-Lot Subdivision:** **Marginally Feasible.** 
    - **Calculations:** 4.02 acres / 4 lots = 1.005 acres per lot. This is extremely tight. Any land dedicated to a shared driveway, road widening, or utility easements would likely push the lots below the 1.0-acre minimum.
    - **Subdivision Type:** Qualifies as a **Minor Subdivision** ("Rural open ditch").
    - **Requirement:** A **Mandatory HOA** is required for 1-acre lot developments in A2 (per 2024 rules).
    - **Lot Width:** 150ft minimum at the building line. 
    - **Building Mandates:** New homes in A2 must be at least 1,400 sq. ft. (heated) and >24ft wide.

## Road & Infrastructure
- **Water:** Public water is required to hit the 1.0-acre lot size. If public water is not available at the street, the minimum lot size reverts to 2.0 acres, making a 4-lot split **Impossible** (max 2 lots).
- **Sewer/Septic:** Likely septic. 4.02 acres would support 4 septic systems *if* the soil is suitable (Level 3 soil study required).
- **Access:** Kathleen Ln is a residential street. A shared driveway or flag-lot configuration would be necessary to avoid a "Major Subdivision" paving requirement.

## Critical Risks
1. **Acreage Precision:** At exactly 4.02 acres, there is NO margin for error. A survey might reveal the actual acreage is slightly less, or easements might eat into the "usable" 1.0-acre minimum per lot.
2. **Public Water Availability:** Must confirm public water is at the property line. Without it, the deal is dead for 4 lots.
3. **Soil Suitability:** 1-acre lots for septic are tight. A Level 3 soil study is mandatory to ensure all 4 lots have sufficient "usable soil" for primary and backup fields.
4. **Off-market Status:** This is not currently for sale. An "off-market" approach (direct mail or door knocking) would be required.
