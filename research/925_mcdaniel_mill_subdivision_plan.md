# Preliminary Subdivision Layout: 925 McDaniel Mill Rd SW

## Project Goal
Subdivide the 5.06-acre western portion of the parcel into 4 residential lots for the Hamlet families, utilizing individual wells and septic systems.

## Site Constraints (Rockdale Well/Septic)
- **Min Lot Size:** 1.0 Acre
- **Min Lot Width:** 150 ft (at the building line)
- **Well-to-Septic Setback:** 100 ft (Critical path for layout)
- **Total Frontage Available:** 578 ft

## Proposed Lot Layout (The "Three-Plus-One" Strategy)

### Side A: The Main Hamlet (Western 5.06 Ac)
To fit 4 families on this side, we must manage the 578 ft frontage carefully. 
- **Option 1: Variance Required**
    - Split into four **144.5 ft** wide lots. 
    - *Pro:* All 4 families are contiguous on the same side of the road.
    - *Con:* Requires a minor variance from the Rockdale Planning Dept (standard is 150 ft for well/septic).
- **Option 2: The "Cluster" Layout**
    - 3 Lots fronting McDaniel Mill Rd at **192 ft** each.
    - The 4th lot is a "Flag Lot" or sits behind the existing structure (requires shared driveway).

### Side B: The Bonus Lot (Eastern 1.38 Ac)
- One oversized 1.38-acre lot.
- *Best Use:* Either the 4th family home (if Side A is too tight) or a communal amenity/sale lot.

## Well & Septic Placement Strategy
To ensure all 4 lots pass Health Dept review:
1. **Wells:** Position all 4 wells along the front property line (near the road) in a staggered pattern.
2. **Septic:** Position all 4 drain fields in the rear of the lots.
3. **Separation:** By keeping all wells front and all septic rear, we maximize the distance (aiming for >150 ft) to ensure no cross-contamination and easier permitting.
