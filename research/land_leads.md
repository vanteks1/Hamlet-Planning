## Detailed Analysis: Candidate 6
- **Acreage Verification:** Hall County Tax records (Parcel ID 15032 000024) confirm a total of 5.02 acres. Historical records or parent parcel data (Parcel ID 15029 000012A) likely caused the 10.5-acre discrepancy.
- **Georgia Power Easement:** The property is encumbered by a high-voltage transmission easement. Plats are available via GSCCCA.org (Hall County records). No permanent structures (houses, barns) can be built within the easement; however, it can be used for septic drain fields (if approved), gardening, or livestock.
- **Septic & Zoning (AR-III):**
    - **Public Water:** Confirmed available via City of Gainesville Department of Water Resources (DWR).
    - **Minimum Lot Size:** Hall County AR-III requires a 1.0-acre minimum lot size for properties on public water and septic.
    - **No Exceptions:** Zoning requirements (1 acre) take precedence over Health Department minimums (0.5 acre). High-quality septic or soil cannot be used to justify lots smaller than the 1.0-acre zoning threshold.
    - **Buildable Area:** Each lot must have 15,000 sq. ft. of "suitable soil" for the primary and replacement septic drain fields. The Georgia Power easement area and road setbacks are excluded from the usable septic area.
- **Subdivision Feasibility:** At 5.02 acres, a 4-lot subdivision (approx. 1.25 acres per lot) is highly feasible given the confirmed availability of public water. The main constraint remains the specific location and width of the Georgia Power easement, which must be verified on a survey to ensure 4 buildable house sites remain.
