# Land Leads: NE Atlanta Metro

This file tracks potential land candidates for the Hamlet project.

## Top Candidates (Initial Search)

### 1. 1272 Old Loganville Rd, Loganville (Gwinnett)
- **Price:** $270,000
- **Size:** 9.08 acres.
- **Pros:** Level land, public water and sewer in place. High subdivision potential.
- **Cons:** Larger than needed; requires major subdivision.
- **Link:** [View on Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/homedetails/1272-Old-Loganville-Rd-Loganville-GA-30052/2061989679_zpid/)

### 2. West Forsyth High District - 5170 Howard Road (Forsyth)
- **Price:** $689,899
- **Size:** 3.48 acres.
- **Pros:** Near Vickery/Milton, sewer additions nearby, located in an existing modern farmhouse enclave. Survey and soil test available.
- **Cons:** Higher price point per acre.
- **Link:** [View on Redfin](https://www.redfin.com/GA/Cumming/5170-Howard-Rd-30040/home/186591873)

### 3. Winder/I-85 Area (Barrow)
- **Price:** $220,000 (Estimated based on area comps)
- **Size:** 2.94 acres.
- **Pros:** Specifically marketed for 2-home split, soil tests done.
- **Cons:** Might be tight for 4 lots (0.7 acre avg), check if 4 is possible.
- **Link:** [View on LandSearch](https://www.landsearch.com)

### 4. Bailey Mill Rd (Forsyth)
- **Size:** 4.92 acres.
- **Pros:** No HOA, lake views, sufficient space for 4-lot split.
- **Cons:** Check sewer vs septic (near lake).
- **Link:** [View on Homes.com](https://www.homes.com) (Search Bailey Mill Rd Forsyth)

### 5. Martin Road Assembly (Jackson)
- **Size:** 251.24 acres (Look for smaller parcels within or nearby).
- **Pros:** Massive road frontage, surrounding estate properties.
- **Cons:** Too large for the group alone, look for "out-parcels" or smaller adjacent lots.
