# Property Research: Lot 4 Stewart Loop, Jefferson, GA

## Executive Summary
**Lot 4 Stewart Loop** is a high-potential 8.82-acre tract in Jackson County. At **$17,500/acre**, it is the most cost-effective option identified that meets the 4-family requirement. However, its success depends on specific soil quality and the legal details of its road maintenance agreement.

---

## 1. Zoning & Dwellings (Jackson County A-2)
*   **Classification:** Agricultural Rural Farm District (A-2).
*   **Primary Dwelling:** One principal single-family residence per lot.
*   **Secondary Dwellings (MIL Suites):** 
    *   **Guest Houses:** Permitted as accessory structures. Must typically be smaller than the main house (often <50% sq ft). 
    *   **Legal Limitation:** To avoid being classified as a "Secondary Residence" (which triggers higher acreage requirements), MIL suites should ideally be designed as guest houses without full separate kitchens (stoves/ovens).
*   **Subdivision:** Minor Subdivision (up to 5 lots) is permitted administratively in Jackson County if each lot meets frontage and acreage minimums.

## 2. Infrastructure & Septic Constraints
*   **Water:** Private Well required (no public water available).
*   **Sewer:** Septic Tank required.
*   **The "1.5 Acre Rule":** Jackson County Environmental Health requires **1.5 acres of usable land per dwelling unit** for properties on wells.
    *   **Group 3 & 4 (Single House):** Need 1.5 acres each.
    *   **Group 1 & 2 (House + MIL Suite):** May technically require **3.0 acres each** if the MIL suite is a full "Second Residence." 
    *   **Mitigation:** By classifying MIL suites as "Guest Houses" sharing a single upsized septic system, the 1.5-acre minimum per lot may still apply.
*   **Soil Requirement:** Each lot must have **25,500 sq. ft.** of "usable soil" (non-floodplain, non-slope).

## 3. Road Maintenance Agreement (RMA)
*   **Type:** Private gravel road (Stewart Loop).
*   **Responsibility:** Proportional cost-sharing for grading and gravel.
*   **Implications:**
    *   **Lending:** Traditional lenders will require a formal, recorded RMA to approve mortgages.
    *   **Usage:** The existing agreement must be checked for "usage caps" or "anti-subdivision" clauses that might restrict adding three additional homes to the road.
    *   **Access:** Fire department access may require widening the road to 20ft at the owner's expense.

## 4. Environmental (Proximity to North Oconee River)
*   **Flood Risk:** Moderate (3/10). 
*   **Constraint:** Lower elevations toward the rear of the property likely fall within the **100-year floodplain**. 
*   **Action:** Building sites must be kept to the cleared/stamped areas on the higher elevations to ensure septic suitability and insurance eligibility.

---

## Strategic Recommendations
1.  **RMA Review:** Request the recorded Road Maintenance Agreement from the listing agent immediately.
2.  **Soil Contingency:** Any offer must include a contingency for a **Level 3 Soil Study** to confirm the land can support 4-6 total septic envelopes.
3.  **Survey/Plat Check:** Confirm with Jackson County Planning if further subdivision of a lot within the "Overlook at River Club" is restricted by existing plat notes.
