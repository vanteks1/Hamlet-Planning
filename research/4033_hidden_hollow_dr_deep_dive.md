## Zoning & Subdivision Feasibility
- **Current Zoning:** **AG-1 (Agricultural)** or **AR-III**
- **Subdivision Requirements:** 
    - **AG-1:** Hall County recently updated its Unified Development Code (Nov 2024), setting the **minimum lot size for AG-1 at 5 acres**. 
    - **AR-III/AR-1:** These districts allow for **1.0 acre** lots if on public water.
- **Feasibility:** 
    - **4-Lot Subdivision:** **LOW FEASIBILITY.** Since the total acreage is 4.24 acres, the property cannot be subdivided within the AG-1 district. 
    - **Rezoning Requirement:** Achieving 4 lots would require rezoning to **AR-1** or **AR-III**. Even then, Hall County Environmental Health requires at least 0.5 - 1.0 acre of "non-restricted" area per lot for septic systems.
    - **Net Usable Area:** The presence of a creek and steep slopes will likely reduce the usable area below the 4.0-acre threshold needed for 4 septic-ready lots.

## Road & Infrastructure
- **Water:** Public water available at the street.
- **Sewer:** Not available. Requires **Septic**. 
- **Soil:** Listing mentions a soil test on file for a 3-bedroom home, but a **Level 3 Soil Evaluation** would be needed for a 4-lot subdivision.

- **Topography:** Sloped and heavily wooded. 
- **Water Features:** **Creek** runs through the property. 
- **Flood Zone:** Likely **Zone A** near the creek banks and **Zone X** (buildable) on the elevated portions.
- **Buffers:** 50ft to 75ft state/county stream buffers will apply, further restricting the buildable "shelf" for 4 separate homes.

## Location & Commute

## Critical Risks
1. **Zoning Barrier:** The 5-acre AG-1 minimum is a "deal-breaker" for a by-right 4-lot subdivision.
2. **Usable Land:** After accounting for the creek, flood buffers, and steep slopes, there may not be enough "non-restricted" land to support 4 separate septic systems and replacement areas.
3. **Commute:** While technically within the 1.25-hour limit, it is on the outer edge of the preferred radius.

## Recommendation
**REJECT / MONITOR ONLY.**
While the price is attractive, the 4.24-acre size is insufficient for a 4-lot split in Hall County's AG-1 district. Rezoning is a high-risk process. This property is better suited for a single large estate or perhaps a 2-lot split if rezoning is successful, which does not meet the project's **4-lot mandate**.
