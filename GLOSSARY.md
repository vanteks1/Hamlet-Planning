# 📖 Glossary of Terms: Land Acquisition & Development

This glossary provides definitions for technical terms used throughout this project to help everyone stay on the same page.

### **1. Zoning & Subdivisions**
*   **Zoning District (e.g., R-100, RA):** A designation given to a piece of land that determines what can be built there and how many houses can fit (density).
*   **Minor Subdivision:** Splitting a lot into a small number of pieces (often 3 or fewer) with a simpler approval process.
*   **Major Subdivision:** Splitting a lot into 4 or more pieces. This usually triggers public hearings and stricter environmental rules.
*   **Plat:** A detailed map showing the boundaries of the land and how it will be divided into individual lots.
*   **Setback:** The minimum distance a house must be from the property line (front, back, and sides).

### **2. Infrastructure & Utilities**
*   **Public Sewer:** A city-maintained waste system. This allows for smaller lots (0.5 acres).
*   **Septic System:** An on-site waste treatment system. Georgia rules usually require a full 1.0 acre of land per septic system.
*   **Road Frontage:** The portion of the property that touches a public road. Each new lot must typically have its own frontage.
*   **Easement:** A legal right for someone else (like a utility company) to use a portion of the land.

### **3. Environmental & Permitting**
*   **FEMA Flood Zone:** An area identified as being at risk for flooding. Building in these zones is either restricted or requires expensive insurance.
*   **LDP (Land Disturbance Permit):** A permit required before you can start clearing trees, grading soil, or building roads.
*   **Stream Buffer:** A protected area of land (often 50-75 feet) next to a creek or river where you are not allowed to build or clear trees.
