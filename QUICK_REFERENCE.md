# Hamlet Planning — Quick Reference Index

This page links to all quick reference documents used in evaluating and executing the 4-family hamlet project. Each document is designed to be actionable — checklists, county-by-county tables, and go/no-go criteria, not general background reading.

---

## Reference Documents

### 1. [Road Frontage & Access Standards](requirements/road_and_access_standards.md)
County-by-county minimum frontage, shared driveway limits, and private road construction requirements across Barrow, Gwinnett, Jackson, Hall, Forsyth, and North Fulton.

**Use when:** Evaluating whether a parcel's road frontage supports a 4-lot subdivision, or determining whether a variance or full subdivision road is required.

---

### 2. [Septic & "Usable Soil" Reference](requirements/septic_and_usable_soil.md)
The #1 reason subdivisions fail in Georgia. Covers the usable soil math per county, per-bedroom drainfield sizing, the impact of MIL suites on septic load, and pre-offer soil feasibility steps.

**Use when:** Assessing whether a parcel has enough qualifying soil to support 4 houses plus up to 4 MIL suites (16+ bedrooms total). Run this check in parallel with zoning — never after.

---

### 3. [ADU & MIL Suite Regulatory Guide](requirements/adu_and_mil_suite_guide.md)
What each county actually allows for secondary dwelling units — attached vs. detached, size caps, kitchen restrictions, owner-occupancy rules, and Special Use Permit requirements. Erica and Gabi's MIL suite requirement is analyzed county by county.

**Use when:** Confirming a target county will allow the type of secondary unit each family needs. Jackson County is currently the most permissive for detached cottages.

---

### 4. [Subdivision Soft Cost & Timeline Estimator](requirements/subdivision_soft_costs_and_timeline.md)
A full cost breakdown from "Raw Land Contract" to "Ready to Pull Building Permits" across four phases: due diligence, subdivision design/approval, infrastructure permitting, and infrastructure construction. Includes a realistic 14–20 month timeline and a red flag escalator table.

**Use when:** Evaluating the true all-in cost of a raw parcel. Budget $50,000–$75,000 per lot in pre-construction costs on top of the land price.

---

### 5. [Utility & Connectivity Checklist](requirements/utility_and_connectivity_checklist.md)
A standard verification protocol for public water, sewer, electric, natural gas, fiber internet, cell coverage, and trash service. Includes a copy-paste parcel scorecard, ISP verification tools, and county utility contacts.

**Use when:** Vetting a specific parcel before making an offer. Run all utility checks before any showing, not after. Fiber is the tiebreaker for otherwise equal parcels.

## Quick Decision Matrix

Use this to quickly screen a parcel before investing time in deeper research.

| Check | Disqualifier | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| Road frontage | < 30 ft with no variance path | 30–60 ft (needs BOA) |
| Usable soil | < 64,000 sq. ft. (4 houses + 2 MIL suites) | < 96,000 sq. ft. (primary + reserve) |
| ADU type allowed | County prohibits detached (for Grandparents/Margaret lots) | SUP required (adds 6–8 weeks) |
| Est. soft costs | > $100k/lot over land price | $60k–$100k/lot over land price |
| Fiber internet | Satellite only | Fixed wireless only |
| Flood zone | AE (high-risk floodplain) | AH / X-shaded |
| Public water | No water within 1 mile | No water at road (extension needed) |
